# RacquetIn Admin — Quick Guide to Adding Products

## Logging In
1. Sign in at your website using your normal account login.
2. Go to your website address + `/admin`.
3. Use the left menu to navigate (Products, Categories, Orders, etc.).

## Adding a Product
1. Go to **Products** → click **+ New Product**.
2. Fill in the fields (see below), then click **Save Product**.

**Required:** Name, Slug (web-friendly name, e.g. `yonex-astrox-100zz`), Category, Price (₹).

**Common optional fields:** Brand, Stock, SKU, Description, Colors/Tags/In Box (comma-separated lists), Warranty.

**Racket-specific (leave blank if not relevant):** Player Level, Playing Style, Balance, Flex, Weight, Frame/Shaft Material.

**Pricing:** Set **Original Price** higher than **Price** to show a sale/discount. Leave blank if not on sale.

**Toggles:**
- **Active** — turns the product on/off on the live site. Must be ON to be visible to customers.
- **Best Seller / New Arrival / Featured** — optional highlight labels.

**Badge** — small label on the product card (Best Seller, New, Sale, etc.), optional.

## Adding Photos
Photos can only be added **after** the product is first saved.
1. Save the product, then reopen it.
2. Click **+ Upload Image**, choose a photo. Repeat for more.
3. Hover a photo to: ★ set as **Primary** (main photo), move it earlier/later, or delete it.

**Tips:** White/plain background, square photos at least 1000×1000px, JPG or PNG, best photo set as Primary.

## Saving & Checking
- Click **Save Product**. A message appears if something required is missing.
- Check the product on your live category page — it must have **Active** turned on.
- Changes appear immediately (refresh if not).

## Editing a Product
1. **Products** → search or open its category → click the product.
2. Edit any field → **Save Product**.
3. Add/remove photos the same way as above.
4. For sizes/options, use the **Variants** section (after saving) — add a Name (e.g. "Size"), Value (e.g. "UK8"), and Stock.
5. Active/Best Seller/New toggles can often be flipped directly from the product list.

## Quick Troubleshooting
- **Not showing on site** → check Active is on, and Category is correct.
- **No photo section** → save the product once first, then reopen it.
- **Wrong price shown** → check Original Price isn't lower than Price.
- **Save won't work** → check the required fields (Name, Slug, Category, Price).
